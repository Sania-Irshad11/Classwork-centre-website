ABOUT CLASSWORK CENTRE

Education With Purpose

A learning centre in Mango, Jamshedpur focused on academic tuition and subject-based learning.

To integrate life and education for development.

Classwork Centre provides tuition classes for students from Nursery to Class XII across different boards.

Students from Nursery to Class X can study all subjects, while Class XI and XII students can receive Maths and Physics tuition.

Special Computer Classes are also offered.
