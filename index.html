About Classwork Centre

Learning today for a better tomorrow.

Classwork Centre is a local tuition and academic support centre located in Mango, Jamshedpur, Jharkhand.


We provide academic guidance and tuition support for students up to 10+2, including students from different boards, subjects and streams.


The centre is led by educator Tauqeer Alam Ansari, with a focus on understanding concepts, regular academic support and helping students build strong foundations.
